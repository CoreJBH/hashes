First public build: solo Stratum V1 mining, monochrome CRT terminal, live hash stream, payout address validation with paste and QR scan, COOL and TURBO modes. The terminal now fills the screen at any height, a WORKERS panel shows each thread's own rate beside hash-rate and temperature sparklines, and a session keeps running while you are in another app, as a foreground service with a live notification and a STOP action.
