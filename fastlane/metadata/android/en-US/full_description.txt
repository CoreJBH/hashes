Hashes is a solo Bitcoin miner that looks like the terminal it is.

It hashes real mainnet block headers from a solo pool, shows every hash as it is produced, and pays any block it finds straight to a Bitcoin address you type or scan. There is no account, no wallet, no seed phrase and no private key: the only thing the app ever asks for is the address that should receive a reward.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
- It does not simulate mining. The nonce counter, the double SHA-256 output and the hash stream are the values the device is actually producing.
- It does not promise a block. Solo mining is a lottery: the odds line on screen is the honest expected time at your current hash rate.
- It does not keep mining after you stop it. Leaving the app keeps the session running, but the button, the STOP action in the status-bar notification, and a swipe out of the recent tasks all end it.

HOW IT MINES
- Assembled from a Stratum V1 solo pool: subscribe, authorize, notify, set difficulty and submit.
- TLS first. The payout address travels in the login line, so a pool that offers Stratum over TLS is dialled over TLS with certificate and host name verification.
- Mining continues while you are in another app, as a foreground service with the live hash rate in the status bar.
- COOL is the default: a short hash window per screen frame keeps the CPU share small and the phone cool, while the terminal still updates at 30 frames per second.
- TURBO uses the ARMv8 SHA-256 instructions and more than one worker thread, and steps back automatically when the device gets hot.
- Found shares are submitted with the job they belong to. A reconnect never reuses a stale job and no share is ever invented to look busy.

THE TERMINAL
- Monochrome phosphor green on near black, one font, scanlines, no decoration that is not information.
- BLOCK_HEIGHT, PREV_HASH, PAYOUT_ADDR, NET_TARGET and NONCE_COUNTER for the job being hashed.
- LIVE_HASH_STREAM shows the last ten hashes; a hash with four or more leading zeros is inverted so a near miss is visible at a glance.
- SESSION_BEST_SHARE and ALL_TIME_BEST keep the best hash this device has produced, the second one across restarts.
- WORKERS is an htop-style bar per worker thread, from the core's own per-worker counters, with TURBO 7T or a thermal CAP, plus live hash rate and temperature sparklines.
- EVENT_LOG is the session log: jobs, shares, link changes and thermal steps, newest first.
- The TURBO line and the START / STOP and POOL & PAYOUT buttons sit at the bottom edge; everything above them stretches to fill the screen.
- POOL & PAYOUT opens the pool presets, a custom endpoint field, and the payout target prompt with paste and QR scan.

PAYOUT ADDRESS
The address is checked before it is used: Base58Check for 1... and 3..., Bech32 for bc1q..., Bech32m for bc1p..., with the checksum verified and testnet addresses refused. A QR code from a wallet is parsed down to the address itself and then checked the same way.

PRIVACY
No analytics, no accounts, no Google Play Services. The only network connection is the pool you chose. The app is open source and builds from source without proprietary dependencies.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 8.0 or newer on a 64-bit ARM device (arm64-v8a). The app ships that one ABI only, because its hash engine is native arm64 code, so a 32-bit ARM or an x86 device cannot install it. On a supported device whose native library fails to load, the app still runs on its slower Kotlin engine.

Mining on a phone is a hobby, not a business. Expect nothing, enjoy the terminal.
